Artist Management Services

We provide every tool, relationship, and strategy an artist needs to build a lasting career — without compromising their vision. No template solutions. No shortcuts.

Detailed service descriptions

01

Career Strategy

Every artist is different. Our career strategy process begins with a deep listening session — understanding where you are, where you want to go, and what makes your art irreplaceable.

We build detailed roadmaps that cover release schedules, touring windows, media moments, and long-term positioning. These aren't generic templates — they're living documents that evolve with your career.

  • Long-range release and touring calendars
  • Competitive landscape analysis and positioning
  • Milestone planning with measurable objectives
  • Quarterly reviews and course corrections

02

Booking & Touring

Performance is where music lives. Our booking team maintains relationships with venues, promoters, and festival programmers across the country and internationally — ensuring our artists get in front of the right audiences at the right moments.

We handle every logistical detail — contracts, riders, routing, travel — so you can arrive focused entirely on the performance.

  • Club, theatre, and festival bookings
  • Contract negotiation and rider management
  • Tour routing and logistics coordination
  • International touring support

03

Brand Development

Your brand is your music made visible. We work with artists to define, refine, and express their identity across every visual and verbal touchpoint — from press photos and artwork to website and social presence.

The best artist brands feel inevitable — like an extension of the music itself. That's what we build.

  • Visual identity and art direction
  • Artist bio and messaging development
  • Website design and social profile optimisation
  • Merchandise and physical product direction

04

Strategic Marketing

Audience growth requires more than posting on social media. We develop data-informed campaigns that reach the right listeners across digital, editorial, and experiential channels — building sustainable fanbases, not fleeting spikes.

  • Release campaign planning and execution
  • DSP playlist pitching (Spotify, Apple Music, etc.)
  • Social content strategy and community management
  • Paid advertising and targeting strategy

05

Press & PR

Earned media coverage changes careers. Our PR team maintains strong relationships with music journalists, bloggers, podcast hosts, and tastemaker platforms. We craft pitches that make editors want to say yes.

  • Press kit creation and media outreach
  • Interview coordination and media training
  • Tastemaker and blog placement
  • Radio and editorial pitching

06

Label & Deal Negotiation

Contracts define careers. We negotiate on behalf of our artists across the full spectrum of deal types — label agreements, sync licensing, brand partnerships, and publishing — ensuring fair terms that respect artistic control and maximise long-term revenue.

  • Label and distribution deal negotiation
  • Sync licensing for film, TV, and advertising
  • Brand sponsorship and endorsement deals
  • Publishing and royalty structure guidance

How We Work

Our Process

Every engagement starts with listening. Here's how we bring an artist into the Wild Music Group family.

  1. 01

    First Listen

    We receive your music through our submission portal. Every submission gets a genuine listen from a team member — no algorithms, no shortcuts.

  2. 02

    Initial Conversation

    If your music resonates, we schedule a conversation — no pitch, no pressure. We want to understand you, your goals, and your vision.

  3. 03

    Strategy Workshop

    Together we build your roadmap: a detailed career strategy covering releases, touring, branding, and growth over the next 12–24 months.

  4. 04

    Partnership

    We formalise our partnership and go to work. You make the music. We make everything else happen.
